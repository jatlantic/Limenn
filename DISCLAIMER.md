# Disclaimer

**Please read this before you rely on limenn for confidential information.**

## No guarantee of complete protection
limenn detects and replaces personal data automatically. Detection is good but **not perfect**: names, numbers, health details or other identifying information can be missed, especially in unusual formats, in casual writing or in scanned documents. limenn reduces the personal data sent to AI services. It **does not guarantee anonymity**. The replacement is technically *pseudonymisation*: the mapping back to the real values stays on your computer. **Always check the cleaned text** (side panel, or *what ChatGPT saw* in the workspace) before you send anything confidential.
