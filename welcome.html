installed · everything runs on this computer

use ChatGPT, Claude and Grok without giving away personal data.

limenn replaces names, numbers and other personal details with placeholders like [PERSON-1] before anything leaves your computer, and puts the real values back into the answer.

  1. 1

    pin limenn to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle piece 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin 📌 next to limenn. Clicking the limenn icon opens a side panel that shows exactly what was protected.

  2. 2

    ask your first question privately

    The easiest and most private way: the workspace, limenn's own tab. Write there, choose ChatGPT, Claude or Grok, and read the answer with your real data filled back in.

    or write directly on the site, in the limenn bar at the top of the page:

    try: I am Maria Keller, AHV 756.1234.5678.97, and need a polite letter to my landlord at Seestrasse 4, 8002 Zürich.

  3. 3

    optional: link a folder of documents

    Contracts, letters, scans: limenn prepares them ahead of time, including scanned pages, and saves a cleaned copy in limenn-prepared/. Attaching them later is instant. Open the side panel and click link vault folder.
